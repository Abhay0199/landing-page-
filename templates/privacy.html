Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and a securely hashed password. As you use the Service, we store the expense data you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We may also collect non-personal usage data such as browser type, device information, and pages visited to help us improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your personal and financial data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly experience. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Display your expense history and spending summaries
  • Authenticate your identity when you sign in
  • Generate category breakdowns and monthly reports
  • Improve the reliability and performance of the Service

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secure database and is accessible only to you through your authenticated account. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, alteration, or destruction.

You may delete individual expense entries at any time. If you wish to delete your entire account and all associated data, please contact us using the details below.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, or font delivery (e.g. Google Fonts). These services may collect limited, non-personal data such as IP addresses in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We do not integrate with any third-party advertising networks or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
